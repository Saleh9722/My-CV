Palestine Explorer: Midterm Challenge

Palestine is a historic land located in the heart of the Middle East known for its deep-rooted history that goes back thousands of years. It is home to many old cities villages and cultural landmarks that reflect a rich and diverse heritage.

Cities such as Nablus Bethlehem Jenin Jericho Tulkarm and Gaza each carry their own unique character from ancient markets and religious sites to fertile farmlands and coastal views. Palestine is also known for its warm hospitality traditional food and vibrant local culture that has been passed down through generations.

This website aims to introduce you to some of the most important Palestinian governorates, giving a glimpse into their history geography and what makes each one special.
